Ride JozEV

A premium electric moped experience designed for hospitality, tourism, and sustainable mobility innovation.

A New Kind of Quiet Luxury

Ride JozEV blends refined design with effortless electric performance. Built for modern hospitality, coastal destinations, and urban environments, its presence is subtle, calm, and unmistakably premium.

Every surface, every sound, and every touchpoint is engineered to feel smooth, intentional, and comforting. Mobility becomes part of the guest experience rather than a disruption to it.

Innovation That Matters

JozEV is more than a vehicle. It is an adaptable platform for low-impact mobility, lightweight energy use, and circular battery lifecycle management. Designed for environments where sustainability and guest comfort must coexist.

  • Low-load micro-charging requiring minimal electrical upgrades.
  • Diagnostic tracking supporting battery reuse and circular recovery.
  • Lightweight EV architecture optimized for resorts and tourist areas.
  • Designed for future fleet software and modular upgrades.

Policy Alignment with the Netherlands and North Holland

Ride JozEV aligns directly with regional and national programs supporting zero-emission mobility, sustainable tourism, and circular energy systems.

  • Supports zero-emission zones and quieter city centers.
  • Micro-charging reduces pressure on electricity networks.
  • Battery lifecycle design contributes to circular economy KPIs.
  • Strengthens sustainable tourism offerings in Dutch regions.
  • Creates pathways for innovation pilots with public agencies.
